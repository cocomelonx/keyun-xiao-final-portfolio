About the project

01 / 08

A coffee bar that serves its menu the way a quartet plays — one body of work, released in four movements.

Monolith is a specialty shop and roastery on East Valley Boulevard in Alhambra, pouring drinks with names like Five Moons and Tiger in Tweed to a room the LA press has called speakeasy-quiet. The identity takes the name literally: a single standing mass, scored by the seasons that divide it. Every surface — cup, card, feed — is a face of the same stone.

The system is bilingual by default. English carries the drink, Chinese carries the chapter, and neither is a translation of the other.

One stone,
four movements

The mark splits as the year does — 序 still, 雨讯 monsoon, 日曜 solstice, 霜降 frost.

印

The mark

02 / 08

Two masses, one tall and one fallen, cut by strata that read as sediment up close and as a bar line from across the room. The fragment is deliberate: a monolith that has given something up is more interesting than one that has not.

Stacked for the cup, where the wordmark has to survive a sleeve and a lid.

色

Palette

03 / 08

Basalt and chalk do the structural work. The warm half of the range is pulled off the counter — crema, clay, the oat-milk haze in a cup held to the window — and only ever appears as a gradient field, never as a flat fill.
