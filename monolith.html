Sichuan Impression — 锦城里, Jin Cheng Li — has been cooking Sichuan food in Los Angeles since 2014. The name joins 锦城, an old name for Chengdu, with 里, meaning alley: the crowded lanes where that city does its eating. The founder grew up in a family of Chengdu restaurateurs and brought the cooking to LA, where it now runs across four restaurants and has held a place in the Michelin Guide six years running, with a Bib Gourmand in 2025 and Alhambra’s Restaurant of the Year in 2024.

Redesigned the website from ideation to handoff while working with back-end team. The new website’s traffic has since increased by 60% and handled 40,000+ online orders.

sichuanimpressions.com

Website features

01Online orderingPickup and delivery handed to the order.online platform from any page, with DoorDash kept as a parallel route.

07Press and awards timelineEvents runs 2014 to 2025 — the Jonathan Gold review, Restaurant of the Year, the Bib Gourmand and the move to the new Alhambra room.
08Story, honors, growthAbout carries the name’s meaning, the founder’s route from Chengdu, the Michelin listings and a growth timeline.
09MenuThe full dish list, shared with the party trays so catering and dine-in never drift apart.
10Social proof and contactInstagram, Yelp and Facebook alongside a direct email, carried in the footer of every page.
11A/B testing built inOptimizely runs on the live site, so layout and copy changes can be measured rather than argued about.
12Built in FramerThe whole site ships from Framer, which keeps the restaurant able to edit hours and events without a developer.

Women in STEM (WiSTEM) is a volunteer-run nonprofit founded in 2017 — by students, for students — on a mission to fight gender inequality in the sciences by getting teenage girls engaged early and keeping them there. Its tagline, a social approach to a fact-oriented field, is also its method: the work happens through chapters students start in their own schools, mentor pairings, speaker nights and local outreach rather than one-off visits.

It now runs on three pillars — education, mentorship and outreach — carried by a student ambassador network that spans US states and a growing international list, with a resource hub of leadership applications, chapter funding and teaching tools behind it. Donations are tax-deductible through its fiscal sponsor, The Hack Foundation, a 501(c)(3).

Redesigned and maintained the organization’s website, updating content and fixing front-end bugs in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Designed 180+ graphics and social media posts in Canva and Photoshop, increasing social media presence from ~2,000 to ~6,000 and interactions by 200% within a year.
